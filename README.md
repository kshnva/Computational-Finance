# Computational Finance

Implementations of numerical methods for option pricing, hedging, and volatility estimation, completed as part of the Computational Finance course at the University of Amsterdam.

**Authors:** Kushnava Singha and Yoad van Praag

## Assignments

### Assignment 1 — Volatility Estimation, Option Pricing, and Hedging

- Estimation of historical and implied volatility with comparison to the CBOE VIX
- Analytical pricing of power options using the Black–Scholes PDE
- Delta-hedging simulation under matched and mismatched volatility scenarios

### Assignment 2 — Asian Option Pricing

- Monte Carlo simulation for pricing arithmetic and geometric Asian options
- Variance reduction techniques and convergence analysis

### Assignment 3 — Vanilla Options and Finite Differences

- Pricing vanilla European options using finite difference methods (explicit, implicit, Crank–Nicolson)
- Comparison of numerical accuracy and stability across schemes

## Tech Stack

- Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
